A small e-commerce company wants a Java program to process customer orders.

Requirement
The system should maintain information about multiple orders.
Each order should have:
	Order ID
	Customer name
	Product name
	Quantity
	Price per item
	Customer type
	
The company has two types of customers:
	Regular Customer
	Premium Customer
A Premium Customer receives a discount based on the order amount:
	Order amount below ₹1,000 → no discount
	₹1,000 to ₹4,999 → 10% discount
	₹5,000 and above → 20% discount
A Regular Customer does not receive this discount.

The system should process every order and display:
	Order details
	Original order amount
	Discount amount
	Final payable amount
	Whether the customer received a discount
After processing all orders, the system should display:
	Total number of orders
	Total sales amount before discounts
	Total discount given
	Total amount collected after discounts
